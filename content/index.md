# Borradores para Máquinas y Pájaros

This is my personal blog: a place for notes, unfinished ideas, project logs, and more formal essays.  

There is no specific topic here. Some posts may be about math, programming, engineering, books, tools, music, or reflections. The point is to avoid having everything perfectly organized and allow them to connect naturally.
  
Over time, I might separate more developed pieces from logs, short entries, and rants. For now, this site is a public notebook.

It is likely that some posts will be made in English while others in Spanish, because of this, **I invite you** to use the translation tool of the web browser you are using.
